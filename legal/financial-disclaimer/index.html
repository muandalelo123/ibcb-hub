Financial Disclaimer

Important information about the business funding and credit card offers presented on iBCB-HUB.

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Important: iBCB-HUB is not a lender and does not make credit decisions. Information about funding and credit cards is general and may change. Always check the current terms with the lender or issuer before applying.

We are not a lender

iBCB LLC does not lend money, issue credit cards, make credit decisions or set rates and terms. Loans, lines of credit, merchant cash advances and credit cards presented on iBCB-HUB are offered by third-party companies. If you apply, your application is reviewed and decided by that company under its own criteria.

Information, not advice

The content on our funding pages is general information to help you compare options. It is not financial, legal, tax or accounting advice, and it does not consider your personal or business situation. Before taking on debt, consider speaking with a qualified professional such as an accountant or financial advisor.

No guarantee of approval or terms

  • We do not guarantee that you will be approved or that you will receive any specific amount, rate, term or approval time.
  • Funding ranges, approval times and other figures shown on the site come from information published by the providers. They are indicative, may be out of date and can change without notice.
  • Products may not be available in every state or to every type of business.

Credit checks

Sending a funding request through iBCB-HUB does not check your credit. When you apply with a lender or card issuer, that company may perform a credit check, which can be a soft or a hard inquiry. A hard inquiry may affect your credit score. Ask the provider before you apply.

Understand the full cost

The cost of business financing varies widely. Some products, such as merchant cash advances and some short-term loans, can be much more expensive than traditional bank loans. Before you accept an offer, make sure you understand the total cost, fees, repayment schedule and any personal guarantee required.

Compensation

We may receive compensation from some lenders, card issuers and partners when you click a link, apply or are funded. This can affect which offers we feature and where they appear. It never changes the terms a lender offers you. See our Affiliate Disclosure.
