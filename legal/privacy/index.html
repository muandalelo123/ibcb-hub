Privacy Policy

What information we collect when you use iBCB-HUB, how we use it, and your choices.

Last updated: November 21, 2026

In short:

  • We collect only what you send us through our forms or by email.
  • We use it to answer you, and never sell it.
  • We do not send your funding request to a lender without asking you first.
  • iBCB-HUB does not use advertising or analytics cookies.
  • You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your information.

1. Who we are

iBCB-HUB (the “site”), available at ibcb-2.com/ibcb-hub, is operated by iBCB LLC, a limited liability company organized in the State of Ohio, United States (“iBCB”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal information we collect on the site, how we use it and the choices you have.

2. Information you give us

We only receive personal information when you choose to send it:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the subject you choose and your message.
  • Funding request form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), monthly revenue range and the funding amount you need.
  • RocketMail early access form: your name, email address and, if you choose, how you plan to use RocketMail.
  • Emails you send us directly, and anything you include in them.

Please do not send sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, bank account details or passwords through our forms or by email. We never ask for them.

3. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the date and time. These server logs are used to keep the site running and secure.

The site does not currently use cookies for advertising or analytics, and does not load third-party tracking scripts. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

4. Links to other websites

The site links to third-party websites, including tool providers, lenders and card issuers. Some of these are affiliate links (see our Affiliate Disclosure). When you click one, the other website may set its own cookies, for example to record that you came from iBCB-HUB. What happens on those websites is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

5. How we use your information

  • To reply to your messages and questions.
  • To review your funding request and email you funding options that may fit your business.
  • To manage the RocketMail early access list and tell you when your access is ready.
  • To improve the site and decide which guides and tools to add.
  • To protect the site against spam, abuse and security threats.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We will not add you to a marketing newsletter unless you ask to be added.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

  • Service providers: companies that host the site and deliver our email process your information on our behalf, only to provide those services.
  • Lenders and partners: we do not send your funding request or contact details to a lender or partner unless you have asked us to, or agreed to it after we asked you.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, our users or the public.
  • Business transfer: if iBCB LLC is involved in a merger, sale or reorganization, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, under the same protections.

7. How long we keep it

We keep messages and requests for as long as we need them to answer you and follow up, and generally no longer than 24 months after our last exchange, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) when the site is accessed securely. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

You can contact us at any time to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to stop contacting you.

Depending on where you live (for example in certain U.S. states, the European Union or the United Kingdom), you may have additional rights under local law. We will respond to requests as required by the law that applies to you. To make a request, email supportbcb@ibcb-s.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it.

10. Visitors outside the United States

The site is operated from the United States. If you use it from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

11. Children

iBCB-HUB is intended for adults running or starting a business. It is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: supportbcb@ibcb-s.com.
