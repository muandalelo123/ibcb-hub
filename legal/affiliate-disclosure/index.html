Affiliate Disclosure

How iBCB-HUB earns money, and what that means for the recommendations you see.

Last updated: November 21, 2026

In short: some links on iBCB-HUB earn us a commission if you sign up or buy. It costs you nothing extra, and it helps keep the site free.

How we earn money

iBCB-HUB is free to use. To support it, iBCB LLC takes part in affiliate and partner programs. When you click certain links to tools, services, lenders or card issuers and then sign up, apply or make a purchase, we may receive a commission or referral fee from that company.

  • No extra cost to you: the price or terms you get are the same whether or not you use our link.
  • Not every link is an affiliate link: we also link to tools and websites we do not earn anything from.
  • Our own products: iBCB RocketMail and the sites of the iBCB Network are owned by iBCB LLC. When we feature them, we have a direct interest in them.

How this affects what we feature

Commissions can influence which offers we choose to feature and where they appear on a page. We aim to recommend only tools and offers we consider genuinely useful for entrepreneurs, and our descriptions are our own opinions. A partner cannot pay to change what we write about its product.

Do your own research

Our recommendations are a starting point, not a substitute for your own judgment. Compare options, read the provider’s terms and choose what fits your business.
