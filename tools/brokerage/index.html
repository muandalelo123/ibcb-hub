FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions entrepreneurs ask most.

Do I need perfect credit to apply?

Not always. Different lenders evaluate businesses differently, and some focus more on revenue, time in business, or funding purpose than on credit score alone.

How fast can I get approved?

Some offers are designed for faster decisions, sometimes within 24 to 48 hours, while others may take longer depending on documentation and lender requirements.

What type of funding can I request?

You can use this page to explore working capital, expansion funding, equipment financing, inventory-related needs, and other business growth use cases.

Is iBCB-HUB a lender?

No. iBCB-HUB helps you compare options and points you to lenders and card issuers. The lender reviews your application and makes the credit decision.

Does sending a request affect my credit score?

No. Our request form does not check your credit. When you apply with a lender, that lender may run a credit check: ask them whether it is a soft or hard inquiry.

Does iBCB-HUB earn money from these offers?

Some links are affiliate links: we may receive a commission if you sign up, at no extra cost to you. This never changes the terms a lender offers you.
